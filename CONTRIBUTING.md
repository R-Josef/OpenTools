# Contributing to OpenTools

## Changelog policy

Public `CHANGELOG.md` entries describe only:

- new user-visible capabilities;
- fixes;
- behavior changes;
- migration or compatibility facts users must know.

Do not add:

- product comparisons or market-positioning analysis;
- internal product strategy or roadmap decisions;
- feature-coverage origin or prioritization rationale;
- implementation-decision background that is not directly useful to users.

Planning material, benchmarks, and decision records are maintained outside this
repository. Keep public changelog text focused on what changed for users.
